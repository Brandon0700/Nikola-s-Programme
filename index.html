    How progression works

    Weeks 1–2: learn technique with comfortable loads. Weeks 3–4: build consistency and work within the rep ranges. Weeks 5–8: make small increases only when ready. Weeks 9–12: consolidate progress and prioritise control.

    For each exercise, if every set reaches the top of its rep range with good form and the weight feels controlled, the app will suggest the smallest available increase next time. It will never force an increase.

    Guidance & recovery

    Cardio & weekly activity

    Cardio supports heart health, stamina, mood and general fitness. It should complement strength training, not compensate for food or force weight loss.

    • Optional 10–15 minutes of comfortable cardio after two gym sessions, or on a separate day.
    • Choose walking, cycling, swimming, dancing or another enjoyable activity.
    • Keep effort moderate: breathing faster but still able to speak in sentences.
    • Reduce or skip cardio if unusually tired, sore or unwell.
    Warm-up principles

    Start with 5–8 minutes of easy movement. You should feel warmer, not exhausted. Complete the session-specific preparation before lifting.

    Food, hydration & sleep

    Eat regular, balanced meals with protein foods, carbohydrates, fruit and vegetables, and healthy fats. Drink water regularly, aim for 8–10 hours of sleep and allow recovery between demanding sessions. Supplements are not required to make progress.

    This programme is written for a 17-year-old. Avoid calorie targets or restrictive diets. The priority is safe technique, strength, energy and healthy development.
    Safety first

    Stop if you feel sharp pain, dizzy or unwell. Ask a qualified gym instructor to check unfamiliar exercises and machine setup. Keep weights controlled and rest around 60–120 seconds between sets, longer after demanding compound lifts.
